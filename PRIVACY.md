# KoVault Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-23_

KoVault is a note-taking app. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data stored on your device
Your notes, folders, labels, drawings, and tasks are stored in the app's local storage on your device.

## Google sign-in and Google Drive (optional)
If you choose to sign in with Google, KoVault receives:
- your **name, email address, and profile picture**, which are shown in the app so you can see which account is connected, and
- permission to create and manage **files that KoVault itself creates** in your Google Drive (the `drive.file` scope). KoVault cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.

KoVault uses this permission only to save a backup of your notes (`KoVault/kovault-vault.json.gz`) in your own Google Drive and to load it back on your devices.

## What we do not do
- KoVault has no servers of its own. Your notes travel directly between your device and Google Drive.
- We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.
- KoVault's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Deleting your data
- **On the device:** uninstall the app or clear its storage.
- **In Google Drive:** delete the `KoVault` folder.
- **Access:** you can remove KoVault's access to your Google account at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## Contact
For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Google Play store listing.
